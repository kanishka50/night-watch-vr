# Credits

Every asset, font and piece of code by other people used in Night Watch. Everything else
(scripts, editor tools, coded shapes, the water and sky shaders, the low-poly clouds, the water
ripple picture and all other sounds, made in code) was made by the team.

The same list, shorter, is on the credits board in the game (results screen, right of the
results; `DebriefPanel.creditsText`). Keep the two the same.
